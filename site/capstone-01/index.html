    Understand

    Capstone 01 — Build a Small Agentic System

    This capstone introduces no new concept. You already have everything from Labs 01–07: model, structured output, tools, knowledge, memory, skills, and the bounded agent runtime. The exercise is choosing — and composing — the smallest justified architecture for one realistic problem.

    Excluding a capability with a good reason counts exactly as much as including one. You probably don't need all of these.

    Canonical version: capstones/01-agentic-system/README.md — the repository stays the source of truth; this page is an interactive layer with local-only progress.

    Understand

    The challenge

    "The notifications service is slow after a release. Investigate what is currently known and prepare a concise engineering handoff. Do not claim a root cause without evidence."

    Everything is fictional (the invented Helio platform), deterministic, and local. Available assets:

    • Service tools — read-only status and deployment fixtures for notifications, billing, search
    • Runbooks — notifications.md, deployment.md, incident-response.md
    • One skill — skills/incident-handoff/SKILL.md
    Available does not mean required. The assets are options, not a checklist.

    Decide

    Architecture decisions

    For each capability: use it or not, why, and the simpler alternative you considered. A justified No is worth exactly as much as a justified Yes — progress here measures decisions made, never capabilities selected.

    MCP is not a decision in this capstone: it has not been taught yet (Milestone 8), and every capability here is local Java.

        Decide

        Decision authority

        Model influence is not decision authority. Whatever architecture you chose, draw this boundary explicitly for your design.

        Model ↓ proposal ↓ validation / policy / authorization ↓ execution

        The concept in full: docs/model-vs-decision-authority.md

        Build

        Build your design

        The browser does not run Maven, and this page will not pretend otherwise. Your work happens in the repository, in the starter module: 

        ./mvnw -pl capstones/01-agentic-system/starter compile exec:java

        Wire your architecture in starter/ (the TODO in StarterMain marks the spot), and record your reasoning in starter/DECISIONS.md — the export on this page can generate it from your answers here.

        Verify

        Verify deterministically

        ./mvnw verify
./mvnw -pl capstones/01-agentic-system/starter test

        Deterministic verification proves your application-controlled behavior: retrieval selection, validation, tool order, bounded stopping. It does not prove the agent's answers are good — that is systematic behavioral evaluation, which the handbook covers later (Milestone 9), and this page will not call it that.
