# Incident Response on Helio

> Helio is a fictional developer platform invented for this handbook. Nothing
> in this document describes a real company or product.

Separate evidence from hypothesis in every incident artifact. Evidence is a
metric, an alert, a timestamp, or a recorded event. A hypothesis is any
explanation that has not been demonstrated.

A handoff must let the receiving engineer continue without asking the author
anything: state what is known, what is suspected, and what remains unknown.

Escalate to the owning team when customer impact is confirmed and no
mitigation is in progress. Do not escalate on hypothesis alone.
